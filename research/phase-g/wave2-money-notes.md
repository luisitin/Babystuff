## Part A
1. `irs-child-tax-credit` (corrected, same id). irs.gov Schedule 8812 and Form 1040-ES results: $2,200 per child for tax year 2026, refundable part $1,700. Child needs an SSN valid for work, issued by the return due date; no SSN gives the $500 Credit for Other Dependents. `whenNote` says plainly that a baby born early 2027 is first claimed on the 2027 return filed in 2028 (late-2026 births on the 2026 return). 2027 amount unknown.
2. `irs-earned-income-tax-credit` (corrected). 2026 one-child limit $51,593 ($58,863 joint), investment income $12,200 (IRS eligibility questionnaire p3524); max $4,427 (Notice 797, shown as Rev. December 2026, a date after today, so noted as a caution). The main 2026 tables page was not in results. `valueBasis` rewritten to say 0 above the limit.
3. `state-paid-family-leave-programs`: REMOVE the old combined id; replaced by 13 per-state entries: ca-paid-family-leave-sdi, nj-family-leave-insurance, ny-paid-family-leave, ri-temporary-caregiver-insurance, wa-paid-family-medical-leave, ma-paid-family-medical-leave, ct-paid-leave, or-paid-leave-oregon, co-famli-paid-leave, dc-paid-family-leave, de-paid-leave, mn-paid-leave, me-paid-family-medical-leave.
   - Maryland: LEFT OUT. Official FAMLI FAQ (April 2026): contributions start Jan 1, 2027, benefits Jan 2028. Leave taken after benefits start and within the child's first year can count even for earlier births.
   - Values are one modest figure: weeks x half the weekly maximum. Maine has none (cap unconfirmed).
   - Conflicts resolved to the lower figure: RI 7 vs 8 weeks (low confidence); DC 8 vs 12 weeks (low confidence; main DC pages say 12); MN $1,423 vs $1,432. Maine low confidence.
   - Application timing was not in results for CA PFL, NJ, NY, RI, WA, MA; left blank in the entry. Stated where found: OR (30 days before to 30 after), CO (30 days before; proof of birth needed), MN (60 days before), DE (as soon as aware).
   - NJ and NY pregnancy disability (TDI) are not valued.
4. `treasury-trump-account-pilot-deposit` (corrected). IRS Form 4547 instructions, FAQ and newsroom results: for births 2025 to 2028 the parent must elect on Form 4547 (Trump Accounts website or app, IRS Individual Online Account, paper, or with a return). Child needs an SSN issued before the election. IRS pages say contributions opened July 4, 2026 and online elections are live; the trustee then contacts the parent. Not automatic. Signup URL changed to irs.gov/trumpaccounts.
